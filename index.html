Our promise

Three rules we don't break

  • No repeats

    A question your child has seen won't come back before Test B. Test B checks understanding, not memory.

  • No filler

    Thousands of questions, carefully picked. Only ones on your child's weak topics make the sheet.

  • No guessing

    If an answer or a question is in doubt, a teacher checks it. Nothing is guessed.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

How does AfterTest know which topics are weak?

Every Test A question is tagged with one topic and a difficulty level. Wrong answers are grouped by topic, so the report card names the exact topics to work on, not just the marks lost.

Will two children in the same class get the same practice sheet?

Usually not. Each sheet has new questions on that child's own weak topics, at a similar level. Topics they already got right are left out.

Is Test B the same for every child?

No. Each child's Test B covers only their weak topics from Test A, with questions they have not seen in Test A or in practice.

What if a topic is still weak after Test B?

The report card marks it "Still weak" and the teacher takes it up again with your child.

Does a good Test B score mean the topic is mastered?

It is strong evidence, not a guarantee. You see real counts, like 0/2 in Test A and 3/3 in Test B, never a vague percentage.

Who is it for?

Students of Physics, Chemistry, Maths and Biology, in CBSE, ICSE and IB schools and preparing for JEE and NEET.
